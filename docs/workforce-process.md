# Workforce leave process and decision principles

Source: stakeholder answers supplied directly by the user during the application redesign.

## Established process

The staff member submits the centrally controlled Excel leave form to HSS. HSS routes it to the supervisor for approval. Approved leave is subsequently reflected on the payslip. Integration with the statewide Excel form is explicitly out of scope. The application's planning and recorded supervisor reviews do not submit HSS forms or update payroll.

Review times depend on complexity: corporate requests may be reviewed immediately; complex clinical rosters may take weeks. Do not present a universal approval SLA or approval probability.

When a submitted form is not approved, a rejection alert email is generated through the existing process. The supervisor handles communication. Informal unsupported discussions may never enter the formal records, so observed records do not establish rejection frequency or complete fairness history.

## Decision order

1. Safety: staffing, skills, workload, other planned leave, applicable policy and medical specialty.
2. Equity: transparent comparable leave history, with final judgement retained by the supervisor.
3. Supporting considerations such as cost, continuity, staff preference and speed.

Same staff type should cover a role where cover is required. Cross-role substitution is generally not allowed. Cross-ward nursing cover can be possible with context-specific checks. Medical specialties are not interchangeable; matching an RMO title and rate is insufficient evidence of specialty eligibility. Corporate roles may not require cover.

Reallocation, casual pool, agency and overtime are potential coverage sources. Their actual availability and cost are not established by the current extract. An uncovered shift is a risk requiring escalation, not an acceptable safety workaround.

Substantive means permanently appointed; acting means temporarily appointed at that level. Acting status alone does not reduce roster role eligibility. Work-code meanings and Employee Overall Job Type remain unclarified; do not invent mappings.

## Policy and fairness evidence

The stakeholder says the nursing ratio policy and award data were supplied. The current app extract contains references but no indexed policy passages. Use 'not indexed in this workspace' rather than claiming no policy was provided. Nursing ratios require profession/unit applicability; do not apply them to the medical RMO case by default.

Peak-period fairness is handled by individual supervisors today. No universal rotation formula or historical allocation record has been established. Keep illustrative rotation controls separate from actual employee prioritisation.

## Presentation

The everyday interface uses natural product language, calendar planning and supervisor review. Technical provenance belongs in Data Sources and expandable evidence cards. Display names are aliases mapped to source identifiers there. No invented clinical roster counts, nurse identities, current balances, confirmed cover acceptances or verified policy results are introduced to match the reference artwork.

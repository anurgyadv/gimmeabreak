## Interpretation boundaries

- A preserved roster headcount is a useful simulation result; it is not proof that patient ratios or clinical competencies are satisfied.
- No mapping from anonymised service units to the named hospitals/wards is supplied by these public policies. Patient counts and direct-care/HFSC assignments are also necessary for a ratio finding.
- 9.5 hours is not a universal shift-rest minimum. Night/day transitions have a separate 20-hour provision; instruments include exceptions and approved flexible arrangements.
- Same-role replacement and safety-before-equity prioritisation come from the stakeholder instructions. Do not attribute those exact rules to an unquoted policy clause.
- A candidate's recorded role is not evidence of current specialist competency, willingness or confirmation. Preserve those separate states.
- No phrase in these documents authorises this app to automatically issue final leave approval or send real messages.
- Original PDFs are retained for source verification. UI copy uses concise paraphrases; the app's index contains no extended verbatim excerpts.

All page numbers in the JSON index are one-based PDF pages, including covers and registration orders; the printed page labels can differ.

## Response deadlines and roster conditions

- ANF clause 33(1)(b–e), PDF page 72: annual-leave response within 14 days from submission; a refusal is written, reasoned and within that period. Operational grounds need specific details.
- AMA clause 34(11), PDF page 46: written confirmation of when annual leave can be taken within two weeks of receipt of the written application. This is a response obligation, not automatic approval.
- ANF clause 28(14–15), PDF page 59: two full days off per week or four per fortnight; seven consecutive duties is qualified by practicability and an employee-request/employer-approval exception. Ten duties per fortnight; ten-hour night duties have separate five-consecutive/eight-fortnight limits. Approved flexible arrangements need their own recorded basis.
- AMA clause 34(14), PDF page 47: short-leave backfill rules differ from nursing. Do not automatically apply nursing coverage logic to doctors.
- Accrued-leave thresholds are entitlement-based. Preserve imported excess flags; a raw hours balance alone cannot establish excess without agreement, FTE and entitlement context.
- The downloaded Nursing and Midwifery Workforce Planning Framework v2, pages 5 and 43, supports local skill mix and service-demand planning; it does not supply a universal senior-to-junior percentage.
